Why I like Kerbal Space Program

What is it that makes Kerbal Space Program unique?

It appeals to so many different types of play styles that this results in a very broad appeal.

So, why am I making a fan page about this game? What do I enjoy in particular? Well... that is hard to sum up but I will try my best. I enjoy simulation games. That is likely obvious from my selection of games on this site. But what is unique about KSP for me is the simulation aspect of the game. Many people love the sandbox and messing with the physics of the game but for me it is all about build a real space program!

KSP screenshot placeholder 1

What do I love? It is the feel of starting from nothing and expanding your space program bit by bit. Improving your programs capabilities with no end. At the start you can barely get out of the atmosphere, but then soon you will have a design that can launch a payload to anywhere on Kerbal, then to the Mun/Minmus and then to new planets in the solar system and finally interstellar!! This is not to even mention creating space stations in orbit and creating planetary bases and colonising the new planets/moons you explore. There are not many games that can give you this feeling of expansion and goals to carry out.

In order to reach long term goals you need to have money and research points. But for enjoyment, it is important to avoid turning the game into a grind. You need to keep it fresh and come up with short term goals and new designs in order to reach your long term goals.

I enjoy that the long-term goals can be so varied: colonisation, tourism, space stations, designing the next interstellar spaceship, designing a planetary lander that does not tip over or simply visiting a new location. With the addition of mods it just makes it endless. With so many things to factor in, you will always be upgrading your designs and tinkering with new mechanics. This site will cover some of my thoughts on how to make your game more fun!
